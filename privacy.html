Privacy policy

Hermes Agent (personal) is a personal automation client used solely by its owner to access the owner's own Google account data (Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Google Docs).

  • No data is collected from, or shared with, any third party.
  • Data is sent only to Google's own APIs, called directly from the owner's Mac.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally with owner-only file permissions.
  • The app is not distributed or offered to other users.
  • Access is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests.

To revoke access: myaccount.google.com > Data & privacy > Third-party apps.
